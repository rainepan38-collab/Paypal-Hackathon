Welcome to SplitMate

A mobile-friendly group chat for your PayPal AI Hackathon demonstration. No WhatsApp or Meta account required.

  1. Chat with your group or switch demo participants.
  2. Send @SplitMate I paid $96 for dinner with Alex and Sarah. Split equally.
  3. Confirm the proposed split to get a payment card for each person.
  4. Open a card and pay using a PayPal Sandbox Personal test account.
  5. Return to the chat to see payment status update automatically.
PayPal Sandbox transfers test funds to the configured sandbox merchant, not to a real friend. This is a demo, not a P2P payment service.
